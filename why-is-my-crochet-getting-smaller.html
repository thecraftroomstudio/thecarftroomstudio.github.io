Why Is My Crochet Getting Smaller?

If your crochet project keeps getting narrower as you work, you may be accidentally losing stitches. Let's find the common causes and how to fix them.

COMMON REASONS

Why is my crochet getting smaller?

If each row seems shorter than the one before it, you're probably losing stitches somewhere. These are some of the most common causes.

01

You're missing a stitch

The first and last stitches of a row are easy to miss. Count your stitches after every row while you're learning.

02

You're skipping the last stitch

The last stitch can hide along the edge of your work. Check carefully before turning your project.

03

You're working into the wrong place

Make sure your hook is going into the correct stitch instead of accidentally skipping a stitch or working into a gap.

04

Your stitch count is changing

Write down how many stitches each row should have. Compare your actual count before starting the next row.

QUICK CHECK

Let's find the missing stitches.

Start by checking the beginning and end of your last few rows. These are common places for beginners to accidentally lose stitches.

Check the beginning → Check the ending → Check your stitch count → Check your tension →

CHECK 01

Check the beginning of the row.

Look carefully at the first stitch of each row. It is easy to accidentally skip it when turning your work.

CHECK 02

Check the end of the row.

The last stitch can be difficult to see, especially for beginners. Before turning your work, make sure you have worked into the correct final stitch.

CHECK 03

Count your stitches.

If your pattern says you should have 20 stitches, make sure you actually have 20 before beginning the next row. Finding a missing stitch early makes fixing the problem much easier.

CHECK 04

Check your tension.

Very tight tension can make your crochet look smaller and harder to work into. Try to keep your grip relaxed and your working yarn comfortable.

STILL STUCK?

Start with stitch counting.

If your crochet keeps getting smaller, learning how to count stitches can help you find exactly where stitches are disappearing.
