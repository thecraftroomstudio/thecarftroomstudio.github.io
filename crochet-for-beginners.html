YOUR FIRST STEPS

What do you need to start crocheting?

You don't need a huge collection of supplies. Start with a few basic tools and learn as you go.

01

Crochet Hook

Choose a beginner-friendly hook that feels comfortable in your hand. A medium-sized hook is a practical place to start.

02

Yarn

Choose a smooth, medium-weight yarn in a light colour. It makes your stitches easier to see while learning.

03

Scissors

A small pair of sharp scissors is enough for cutting your yarn.

04

Yarn Needle

A yarn needle helps you neatly hide the loose ends when your project is finished.

BEGINNER BASICS

Learn these skills first.

You don't need to learn every crochet stitch at once. Focus on a few fundamentals and build your confidence gradually.

How to make a slip knot → How to crochet a chain → How to make a basic stitch → How to count stitches →

SKILL 01

How to Make a Crochet Slip Knot

The slip knot is usually the first step before making your foundation chain. Practice making it loose enough for your hook to move comfortably.

SKILL 02

How to Crochet a Chain Stitch

The chain creates the foundation for many beginner crochet projects. Practice making several chains with a consistent tension.

SKILL 03

Basic Crochet Stitches for Beginners

Once you are comfortable making chains, you can begin practising basic stitches. Work slowly and focus on keeping your tension consistent.

SKILL 04

How to Count Crochet Stitches

Counting your stitches helps prevent projects from unexpectedly getting wider or narrower. Count your stitches at the end of each row while you're learning.

YOUR FIRST PROJECT

Start small.

Your first project doesn't need to be complicated. A small flower, bow or coaster can help you practise your new skills without feeling overwhelmed.
