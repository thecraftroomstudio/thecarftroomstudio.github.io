How to Crochet a Chain Stitch for Beginners

The chain stitch is one of the first skills you'll use in crochet. Learn it slowly, one loop at a time.

Learn the Chain

STEP BY STEP

Making your first chain.

Don't worry about making it perfect. The goal is to create even loops.

01

Make a slip knot

Place the slip knot on your crochet hook and leave enough room for the loop to move easily.

02

Yarn over

Bring the working yarn over the hook from back to front.

03

Pull through

Use the hook to pull the yarn through the loop already on your hook.

04

Repeat

Keep making yarn overs and pulling through the loop until your chain is the length you need.

CHAIN STITCH TROUBLESHOOTING

Is your chain not looking right?

A few small tension problems can make a chain difficult to work with.

My chain is too tight → My chain is too loose → My chain looks uneven → I keep losing count →

PROBLEM 01

Chain is too tight.

If your hook struggles to enter the chain, your loops may be too small. Try relaxing your grip and pulling the yarn through without tightening the new loop.

PROBLEM 02

Chain is too loose.

If your chain has very large loops, try keeping your hook movement consistent. Avoid pulling the working yarn too far after each stitch.

PROBLEM 03

Chain looks uneven.

Uneven chains are completely normal when you're learning. Practice making several short chains and focus on keeping each loop roughly the same size.

PROBLEM 04

I keep losing count.

Count each completed chain as you make it. For practice, make a chain of 10 and touch each chain with your finger while counting.
