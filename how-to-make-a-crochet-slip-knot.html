CROCHET BASICS • SKILL 01

How to make a crochet slip knot.

A slip knot is one of the first things you'll learn when starting crochet. Once you understand the movement, it becomes quick and easy to make.

STEP BY STEP

Make your first slip knot.

Use a short length of yarn and practise slowly. Don't worry about making it perfect on your first attempt.

01

Place the yarn over itself to create a simple loop. Keep the working yarn and loose end easy to identify.

02

Pull a small section of yarn through the loop to create another loop.

03

Place it on your hook

Put your crochet hook through the new loop and gently pull the yarn to adjust it.

04

Keep it comfortable

The knot should slide along the hook without being extremely tight. You should still be able to move the hook comfortably.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Is your slip knot giving you trouble?

Most beginner problems come from pulling the yarn too tightly or accidentally tightening the wrong part of the knot.

My knot is too tight → My knot keeps coming undone → I can't get my hook through → I keep making it wrong →

PROBLEM 01

Is the knot too tight?

Try loosening the loop before placing it on your hook. The loop should be snug enough to stay on the hook but loose enough to slide.

PROBLEM 02

Does the knot keep coming undone?

Make sure you are pulling the correct strand of yarn when tightening the knot. Practise slowly until you can identify how the loop is formed.

PROBLEM 03

Can't get your hook through?

Your loop may simply be too small. Loosen it slightly and place the hook through before adjusting the tension.

PROBLEM 04

Keep practising.

Making a slip knot may feel awkward at first. Make several practice knots without starting a project. Your hands will become more familiar with the movement.
