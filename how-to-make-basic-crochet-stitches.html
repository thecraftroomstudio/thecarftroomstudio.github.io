THE BASICS

Three things to understand first.

Crochet stitches become much easier when you understand where your hook goes and what each stitch looks like.

01

Find the stitch

Most crochet stitches have a small V-shaped top. This is usually where you insert your hook when working the next row.

02

Insert your hook

Insert the hook into the correct part of the stitch without splitting the yarn.

03

Keep your tension steady

Try not to pull the yarn too tightly. Your hook should move through the loops comfortably.

STITCH 01

Single crochet.

Single crochet is one of the easiest basic stitches to learn. It creates a firm fabric and is useful for many beginner projects.

01

Insert the hook

Insert your hook into the next stitch.

02

Yarn over

Wrap the working yarn over your hook.

03

Pull up a loop

Pull the yarn through the stitch. You should now have two loops on your hook.

04

Pull through both

Yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook.

STITCH 02

Double crochet.

Double crochet creates a taller stitch and a softer, more open fabric than single crochet.

01

Yarn over

Wrap the working yarn around your hook before inserting it into the stitch.

02

Insert the hook

Insert your hook into the next stitch.

03

Pull up a loop

Yarn over and pull through the stitch. You should have three loops on your hook.

04

Work the loops

Yarn over and pull through two loops. Then yarn over again and pull through the remaining two loops.

COMMON PROBLEMS

Why doesn't my stitch look right?

Don't worry if your first rows look uneven. These are the problems beginners commonly run into.

My stitches are too tight → My stitches are too loose → My crochet has unexpected holes → I'm losing stitches →

PROBLEM 01

Stitches are too tight.

Try relaxing your grip on the yarn and hook. Your working loop should be able to move along the hook without being squeezed tightly.

PROBLEM 02

Stitches are too loose.

Keep your hook movement consistent and avoid pulling the working yarn too far after making each stitch.

PROBLEM 03

Unexpected holes.

Check whether you're accidentally skipping stitches or inserting your hook into the wrong place. Count your stitches at the end of every row while you're learning.

PROBLEM 04

I'm losing stitches.

The first and last stitches of a row are easy to miss. Place a stitch marker in the first or last stitch while practicing.
