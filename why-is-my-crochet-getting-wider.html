COMMON REASONS

Why is my crochet getting wider?

If each row seems longer than the one before it, you're probably adding stitches somewhere. Here are the most common places to check.

01

You're adding an extra stitch

An extra stitch can easily be added near the beginning or end of a row. Count your stitches after every row while you're learning.

02

You're working into the turning chain

Depending on the pattern, the turning chain may or may not count as a stitch. Follow the pattern instructions carefully.

03

You're working into the wrong place

Make sure your hook is going into the correct stitch instead of accidentally working into a gap or space beside it.

04

Your stitch count is changing

Write down the number of stitches you should have in each row. Then compare your actual count before starting the next row.
